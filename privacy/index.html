Your privacy.

Prismahedral does not collect personal data, track you across apps or websites, or include advertising or third-party analytics.

Settings stay on your device

Your dice, appearance and behavior settings are stored locally. Recent roll history is kept in the current app session. No app account is required.

Premium purchases

Prismahedral Premium is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app checks Apple’s verified purchase information to unlock Premium and restore eligible purchases. Gary Haus does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase data under its own privacy policy.

Device features

Motion input can be used for shake-to-roll. Sound and haptics are optional. These features run on your device; the app does not upload motion input or roll results.

Support

If you email garyhaus@garyhaus.com, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending payment details or other sensitive information.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
