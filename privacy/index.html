Your privacy.

Prismahedral does not collect personal data, track you across apps or websites, or include advertising or third-party analytics.

Settings stay on your device

Your dice, appearance and behavior settings stay on your device. Locally saved game data can include saved looks, favorite setups, roll recipes, pins, game sessions and notes, roll history and roll-card images. Use the app’s controls to delete saved items and clear roll history. If you share a result or export a preset, the selected content goes to the app, person or location you choose through system share or file controls. Prismahedral does not upload that content to a developer-operated service. No app account is required.

Premium purchases

Prismahedral Premium is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app checks Apple’s verified purchase information to unlock Premium and restore eligible purchases. Gary Haus does not receive your payment card details. Apple handles purchase data under its own privacy policy.

Device features

Roll by double-tapping the dice scene or using its accessible Roll action. Sound and haptics are optional. Rolls and feedback are processed on your device.

Support

If you email garyhaus@garyhaus.com, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending payment details or other sensitive information.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change.
