Get in touch

Questions, ideas or a problem? Email garyhaus@garyhaus.com. Include your device model, iOS/iPadOS version, app version and what happened.

Roll and explore

Double-tap the scene to roll. Single-tap to hide or reveal the controls. Choose a die type and quantity with the dice button; use the palette for appearance, the sun for lighting, and Settings for behavior, view and camera options. Pinch to zoom and use two fingers to adjust the view.

What is free?

Every die type is free: D4, D6, D8, D10, D12, D20 and percentile-tens D100 (00–90). Roll one clear, uncolored crystal die on Frostfall Mountains. Lighting and bloom controls are included.

What does Premium unlock?

One purchase adds more dice, finishes and colors, all four maps, eight surfaces, weather and glint controls. Roll up to ten of one type; D12 and D20 support up to two. There is no subscription.

Restore Premium

Open Settings → Prismahedral Premium, then choose Restore Purchases. A locked option also opens the Premium screen. Use the Apple Account associated with the purchase, or an eligible Family Sharing purchase. Apple may ask you to authenticate. If restoration fails, check your connection and try again.

Layouts and motion

The app supports portrait and landscape on iPhone and iPad. On iPhone Duo, layouts adapt to system-reported display divisions. Settings includes a manual Desk layout. Reduce Motion limits automatic motion and effects.
